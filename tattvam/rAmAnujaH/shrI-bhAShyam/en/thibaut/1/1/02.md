## Appendix: Translation Mismatches & Untranslated Sections

1. **Untranslated Sanskrit Sentence:** 
   The Sanskrit sentence `तद्गुणसंविज्ञानो बहुव्रीहिः।` (which occurs right after `जन्मादीति – सृष्टिस्थितप्रलयम्।`) has no corresponding translation in the English text block provided. A clarifying English note and translation have been suggested inside the notes block for this sentence.
2. **Brief / Omitted Translation:** 
   The short sentence `विशेषणानां व्यावर्तकत्वात्।` ("For attributes are distinctive") is represented only cursorily or as part of the surrounding discourse in Thibaut's overall text. It has been aligned with a literal representation ("For attributes are distinctive.") for clarity.
3. **Extra English Text:**
   The last paragraph of the provided English text introducing the subsequent *adhikaraṇa*:

   is an introductory passage for Sūtra 1.1.3 (*śāstrayonitvāt*). There is no matching Sanskrit source segment for this paragraph in the input text block, which terminates with the colophon `इति श्रीशारीरकमीमांसाभाष्ये जन्माद्यधिकरणम्॥२॥`.
