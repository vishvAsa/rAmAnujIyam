## वेदान्तानां ब्रह्मणि अप्रमाणत्वाशङ्का

<details><summary>English</summary>

The Vedāntin aiming to ascertain the nature of Brahman from Scripture,  
need not be disconcerted by the Mīmāṁsā-theory of all speech having informing power with regard to actions only.
</details>
<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तत्र पूर्व-पक्ष-वादी मन्यते वृद्ध-व्यवहाराद् अन्यत्र शब्दस्य बोधकत्व-शक्त्य्-अवधारणासम्भवात्, व्यवहारस्य च कार्य-बुद्धि-परत्वेन कार्यार्थ एव शब्दस्य प्रामाण्यम् इति कार्य-रूप एव वेदार्थः।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

Here another primā facie view [^fn_26] finally presents itself. The power of words to denote things cannot be ascertained in any way but by observing the speech and actions of experienced people. Now as such speech and action always implies the idea of something to be done (kārya), words are means of knowledge only with reference to things to be done; and hence the matter inculcated by the Veda also is only things to be done.

[^fn_26]: 148:1 This view is held by the Prābhākara Mīmāṁsakas.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तत्र पूर्वपक्षवादी मन्यते वृद्धव्यवहारादन्यत्र शब्दस्य बोधकत्वशक्त्यवधारणासम्भवात्, व्यवहारस्य च कार्यबुद्धिपरत्वेन कार्यार्थ एव शब्दस्य प्रामाण्यमिति कार्यरूप एव वेदार्थः।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अतो न वेदान्ताः परिनिष्पन्ने परे ब्रह्मणि प्रमाण-भावम् अनुभवितुम् अर्हान्ति।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

From this it follows that the Vedānta-texts cannot claim the position of authoritative means of knowledge with regard to Brahman, which is (not a thing to be done but) an accomplished fact.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अतो न वेदान्ताः परिनिष्पन्ने परे ब्रह्मणि प्रमाणभावमनुभवितुमर्हान्ति।
</details>

## सिद्धेऽर्थे शब्दस्य व्युत्पत्तिरित्यंशे भाट्टोक्तोदाहरणनिरासः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

न च पुत्र-जन्मादि-सिद्ध-वस्तु-विषय-वाक्येषु हर्ष-हेतूनां काल-त्रय-वर्तिनाम् अर्थानाम् आनन्त्यात् सुलग्न-सुख-प्रसवादि-हर्ष-हेत्व्-अर्थान्तरोपनिपात-सम्भावनया च प्रियार्थ-प्रतिपत्ति-निमित्त-मुख-विकासादि-लिङ्गेनार्थ-विशेष-बुद्धि-हेतुत्व-निश्चयः,
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

Against this view it must not be urged that in the case of sentences expressive of accomplished facts – as e.g. that a son is born to somebody – the idea of a particular thing may with certainty be inferred as the cause of certain outward signs – such as e.g. a pleased expression of countenance – which are generally due to the attainment of a desired object; for the possible causes of joy, past, present, and future, are infinite in number, and in the given case other causes of joy, as e.g. the birth having taken place in an auspicious moment, or having been an easy one, &c., may easily be imagined.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

न च पुत्रजन्मादिसिद्धवस्तुविषयवाक्येषु हर्षहेतूनां कालत्रयवर्तिनामर्थानामानन्त्यात् सुलग्नसुखप्रसवादिहर्षहेत्वर्थान्तरोपनिपातसम्भावनया च प्रियार्थप्रतिपत्तिनिमित्तमुखविकासादि-लिङ्गेनार्थविशेषबुद्धिहेतुत्वनिश्चयः,
</details>

## सिद्धेऽर्थे शब्दस्य व्युत्पत्तिरित्यंशे नैयायिकोक्तोदाहरणव्युदासः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

नापि व्युत्पन्नेतर-पद-विभक्त्य्-अर्थस्य पदान्तरार्थ-निश्चयेन प्रकृत्य्-अर्थ-निश्चयेन वा शब्दस्य सिद्ध-वस्तुन्य् अभिधान-शक्ति-निश्चयः; ज्ञात-कार्याभिधायि-पद-समुदायस्य तद्-अंश-विशेष-निश्चय-रूपत्वात् तस्य ॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

Nor, again, can it be maintained that the denotative power of words with regard to accomplished things may be ascertained in the way of our inferring either the meaning of one word from the known meaning of other words, or the meaning of the radical part of a word from the known meaning of a formative element; for the fact is that we are only able to infer on the basis of a group of words known to denote a certain thing to be done, what the meaning of some particular constituent of that group may be.--
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

नापि व्युत्पन्नेतरपदविभक्त्यर्थस्य पदान्तरार्थनिश्चयेन प्रकृत्यर्थनिश्चयेन वा शब्दस्य सिद्धवस्तुन्य् अभिधानशक्तिनिश्चयः; ज्ञातकार्याभिधायिपद-समुदायस्य तदंशविशेषनिश्चयरूपत्वात् तस्य ॥
</details>

## सिद्धार्थव्युत्पत्तिविषये अद्वैत्युक्तो[[दारहण|दाहरण]]निरासः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

न च सर्पाद्-भीतस्य नायं सर्पो रज्जुर् एषा इति शब्द-श्रवण-समनन्तरं भय-निवृत्ति-दर्शनेन सर्पाभाव-बुद्धि-हेतुत्व-निश्चयः; अत्रापि निश्चेष्टं निर्विशेषम् अचेतनम् इदं वस्त्व् इति इत्य्-आद्य्-अर्थ-बोधेषु बहुषु भय-निवृत्ति-हेतुषु सत्सु विशेष-निश्चयायोगात्।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

Nor, again, when a person, afraid of what he thinks to be a snake, is observed to dismiss his fear on being told that the thing is not a snake but only a rope, can we determine thereby that what terminates his fear is the idea of the non-existence of a snake. For there are many other ideas which may account for the cessation of his fear – he may think, e.g., 'this is a thing incapable of moving, devoid of poison, without consciousness'--the particular idea present to his mind we are therefore not able to determine.--
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

न च सर्पाद्भीतस्य नायं सर्पो रज्जुरेषा इति शब्दश्रवणसमनन्तरं भयनिवृत्तिदर्शनेन सर्पाभावबुद्धिहेतुत्वनिश्चयः; अत्रापि निश्चेष्टं निर्विशेषमचेतनमिदं वस्त्वित्याद्यर्थबोधेषु बहुषु भयनिवृत्तिहेतुषु सत्सु विशेषनिश्चयायोगात्।
</details>

## पदानाम् अन्यान्वितस्वार्थमात्रबोधकत्वमित्येतानन्निरासः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

कार्य-बुद्धि-प्रवृत्ति-व्याप्ति-बलेन शब्दस्य प्रवर्तकार्थावबोधित्वम् उपगतम् इति, सर्व-पदानां कार्य-परत्वेन, सर्वैः पदैः कार्यस्यैव विशिष्टस्य प्रतिपादनान् नान्यान्वित-स्वार्थ-मात्रे पद-शक्ति-निश्चयः ॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

The truth is that from the fact of all activity being invariably dependent on the idea of something to be done, we learn that the meaning which words convey is something prompting activity. All words thus denoting something to be done, the several words of a sentence express only some particular action to be performed, and hence it is not possible to determine that they possess the power of denoting their own meaning only, in connexion with the meaning of the other words of the sentence.--
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

कार्यबुद्धिप्रवृत्तिव्याप्तिबलेन शब्दस्य प्रवर्तकार्थावबोधित्वमुपगतमिति, सर्वपदानां कार्यपरत्वेन, सर्वैः पदैः कार्यस्यैव विशिष्टस्य प्रतिपादनान्नान्यान्वितस्वार्थमात्रे पदशक्तिनिश्चयः ॥
</details>

## इष्टसाधनत्वं प्रवर्तकोऽर्थः इति मतनिरासः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इष्ट-साधनता-बुद्धिस् तु कार्य-बुद्धि-द्वारेण प्रवृत्ति-हेतुः; न स्व-रूपेण, अतीतानागत-वर्तमान- इष्टोपाय-बुद्धिषु प्रवृत्त्य्-अनुपलब्धेः।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

(Nor must it be said that what moves to action is not the idea of the thing to be done, but the idea of the means to do it; for) the idea of the means to bring about the desired end causes action only through the idea of the thing to be done, not through itself; as is evident from the fact that the idea of means past, future, and even present (when divorced from the idea of an end to be accomplished), does not prompt to action.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इष्टसाधनताबुद्धिस्तु कार्यबुद्धिद्वारेण प्रवृत्तिहेतुः; न स्वरूपेण, अतीतानागतवर्तमान- इष्टोपायबुद्धिषु प्रवृत्त्यनुपलब्धेः।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इष्टोपायो हि मत्-प्रयत्नाद् ऋते न सिद्ध्यति; अतो मत्-कृति-साध्य इति बुद्धिर् यावन् न जायते; तावन् न प्रवर्तते ॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

As long as a man does not reflect 'the means towards the desired end are not to be accomplished without an effort of mine; it must therefore be accomplished through my activity'; so long he does not begin to act.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इष्टोपायो हि मत्प्रयत्नादृते न सिद्ध्यति; अतो मत्कृतिसाध्य इति बुद्धिर्यावन्न जायते; तावन्न प्रवर्तते ॥
</details>

## कार्यार्थस्यैव शब्दवाच्यतापूर्वपक्षनियमनम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अतः कार्य-बुद्धिर् एव प्रवृत्ति-हेतुर् इति प्रवर्तकस्यैव शब्द-वाच्यतया कार्यस्यैव वेद-वेद्यत्वात् परिनिष्पन्न-रूप-ब्रह्म-प्राप्ति-लक्षणानन्त-स्थिर-फलाप्रतिपत्तेः अक्षय्यं ह वै चातुर्मास्य-याजिनः सुकृतं भवति (आ.श्रौ.सू.२.१.१) इत्य्-आदिभिः कर्मणाम् एव स्थिर-फलत्व-प्रतिपादनाच् च कर्म-फलाल्पास्थिरत्व-ब्रह्म-ज्ञान-फलानन्त-स्थिरत्व-ज्ञान-हेतुको ब्रह्म-विचारारम्भो न युक्तः – इति॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

What causes activity is thus only the idea of things to be done; and as hence words denote such things only, the Veda also can tell us only about things to be done, and is not therefore in a position to give information about the attainment of an infinite and permanent result, such result being constituted by Brahman, which is (not a thing to be done, but) an accomplished entity. The Veda does, on the other hand, actually teach that mere works have a permanent result ('Imperishable is the merit of him who offers the cāturmāsya-sacrifices,' and so on); and hence it follows that to enter on an enquiry into Brahman for the reason that the knowledge of Brahman has an infinite and permanent result, while the result of works is limited and non-permanent, is an altogether unjustified proceeding.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अतः कार्यबुद्धिरेव प्रवृत्तिहेतुरिति प्रवर्तकस्यैव शब्दवाच्यतया कार्यस्यैव वेदवेद्यत्वात् परिनिष्पन्नरूपब्रह्मप्राप्ति-लक्षणानन्तस्थिरफलाप्रतिपत्तेः अक्षय्यं ह वै चातुर्मास्ययाजिनः सुकृतं भवति (आ.श्रौ.सू.२.१.१) इत्यादिभिः कर्मणामेव स्थिरफलत्वप्रतिपादनाच्च कर्मफलाल्पास्थिरत्व-ब्रह्मज्ञानफलानन्तस्थिरत्वज्ञानहेतुको ब्रह्मविचारारम्भो न युक्तः – इति॥
</details>

**(सिद्धान्तप्रतिपादन[[ारम्भॉः|ारम्भः]])**

## तत्र कार्ये एव शब्दव्युत्पत्तिरित्यत्र प्रामाणिकासम्मतिः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अत्राभिधीयते – निखिल-लोक-विदित-शब्दार्थ-सम्बन्धावधारण-प्रकारम् अपनुद्य [[सर्व|सर्व-]]शब्दानाम् अलौकिकैकार्थावबोधित्वावधारणं प्रामाणिका न बहुमन्वते ॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

To this we make the following reply.--To set aside the universally known mode of ascertaining the connexion of words and their meanings, and to assert that all words express only one non-worldly meaning (viz. those things to be done which the Veda inculcates), is a proceeding for which men paying due attention to the means of proof can have only a slight regard.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अत्राभिधीयते – निखिललोकविदितशब्दार्थसम्बन्धावधारणप्रकारमपनुद्य [[सर्व|सर्व-]]शब्दानाम् अलौकिकैकार्थावबोधित्वावधारणं प्रामाणिका न बहुमन्वते ॥
</details>

## बुद्धिपूर्वकव्युत्पत्त्या सिद्धेऽपि शब्दशक्तिग्रहः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

एवं किल बालाश् शब्दार्थ-सम्बन्धम् अवधारयन्ति – मातापितृ-प्रभृतिभिर् अम्बा-तात-मातुलादीन् शशि-पशु-नर-मृग-पक्षि-सर्पादींश् च एनम् अवेहि, इमं चावधारय इत्य् अभिप्रायेण अङ्गुल्या निर्दिश्य निर्दिश्य तैस् तैश् शब्दैस् तेषु-तेष्व् अर्थेषु बहुशश् शिक्षिताश् शनैश्-शनैस् तैस् तैर् एव शब्दैस् तेषु-तेष्व् अर्थेषु स्वात्मनां बुद्ध्य्-उत्पत्तिं दृष्ट्वा शब्दार्थयोस् सम्बन्धान्तरादर्शनात् सङ्केतयितृ-पुरुषाज्ञानाच् च तेष्व् अर्थेषु तेषां शब्दानां प्रयोगो बोधकत्व-निबन्धन इति निश्चिन्वन्ति ॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

A child avowedly learns the connexion of words and meanings in the following way. The father and mother and other people about him point with the finger at the child's mother, father, uncle, &c, as well as at various domestic and wild animals, birds, snakes, and so on, to the end that the child may at the same time pay attention to the terms they use and to the beings denoted thereby, and thus again and again make him understand that such and such words refer to such and such things. The child thus observing in course of time that these words of themselves give rise to certain ideas in his mind, and at the same time observing neither any different connexion of words and things, nor any person arbitrarily establishing such connexion, comes to the conclusion that the application of such and such words to such and such things is based on the denotative power of the words.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

एवं किल बालाश्शब्दार्थसम्बन्धम् अवधारयन्ति – मातापितृप्रभृतिभिरम्बातातमातुलादीन् शशिपशुनरमृगपक्षिसर्पादींश्च एनमवेहि, इमं चावधारय इत्यभिप्रायेण अङ्गुल्यानिर्दिश्य निर्दिश्य तैस्तैश्शब्दैस्तेषुतेष्वर्थेषु बहुशश्शिक्षिताश्शनैश्शनैस्तैस्तैरेव शब्दैस्तेषुतेष्वर्थेषु स्वात्मनां बुद्ध्युत्पत्तिं दृष्ट्वा शब्दार्थयोस्सम्बन्धान्तरादर्शनात् सङ्केतयितृपुरुषाज्ञानाच्च तेष्वर्थेषु तेषां शब्दानां प्रयोगो बोधकत्वनिबन्धन इति निश्चिन्वन्ति ॥
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पुनश् च व्युत्पन्नेतर-शब्देषु अस्य शब्दस्यायम् अर्थः इति पूर्व-वृद्धैश् शिक्षितास् सर्व-शब्दानाम् अर्थम् अवगम्य पर-प्रत्यायनाय तत्तद्-अर्थावबोधि वाक्य-जातं प्रयुञ्जते।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

And being taught later on by his elders that other words also, in addition to those learned first, have their definite meaning, he in the end becomes acquainted with the meanings of all words, and freely forms sentences conveying certain meanings for the purpose of imparting those meanings to other persons.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

पुनश्च व्युत्पन्नेतरशब्देषु अस्य शब्दस्यायमर्थः इति पूर्ववृद्धैश्शिक्षितास्सर्वशब्दानाम् अर्थमवगम्य परप्रत्यायनाय तत्तदर्थावबोधि वाक्यजातं प्रयुञ्जते।
</details>

## यादृच्छिक्या व्युत्पत्त्याऽपि सिद्धेऽपि शब्दशक्तिग्रहः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्रकारान्तरेणापि शब्दार्थ-सम्बन्धावधारणं सुशकम् – केनचित् पुरुषेण हस्त-चेष्टादिना पिता ते सुखम् आस्ते इति देवदत्ताय ज्ञापय इति प्रेषितः कश्चित् तज्-ज्ञापने प्रवृत्तः पिता ते सुखम् आस्ते इति शब्दं प्रयुङ्क्ते।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

And there is another way also in which the connexion of words and things can easily be ascertained. Some person orders another, by means of some expressive gesture, to go and inform Devadatta that his father is doing well, and the man ordered goes and tells Devadatta 'Your father is doing well.'
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्रकारान्तरेणापि शब्दार्थसम्बन्धावधारणं सुशकम् – केनचित्पुरुषेण हस्तचेष्टादिना पिता ते सुखमास्ते इति देवदत्ताय ज्ञापय इति प्रेषितः कश्चित् तज्ज्ञापने प्रवृत्तः पिता ते सुखमास्ते इति शब्दं प्रयुङ्क्ते।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

पार्श्व-स्थो ऽन्यो व्युत्पित्सुर् मूकवच् चेष्टा-विशेषज्ञस् तज्-ज्ञापने प्रवृत्तम् इमं ज्ञात्वाऽनुगतस् तज्-ज्ञापनाय प्रयुक्तम् इमं शब्दं श्रुत्वा अयं शब्दस् तद्-अर्थ-बुद्धि-हेतुः इति निश्चिनोति इति कार्यार्थ एव व्युत्पत्तिर् इति निर्बन्धो निर्निबन्धनः।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

A by-stander who is acquainted with the meaning of various gestures, and thus knows on what errand the messenger is sent, follows him and hears the words employed by him to deliver his message: he therefore readily infers that such and such words have such and such a meaning.--We thus see that the theory of words having a meaning only in relation to things to be done is baseless.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

पार्श्वस्थोऽन्यो व्युत्पित्सुर्मूकवच्चेष्टाविशेषज्ञस्तज्ज्ञापने प्रवृत्तमिमं ज्ञात्वाऽनुगतस्तज्ज्ञापनाय प्रयुक्तमिमं शब्दं श्रुत्वा अयं शब्दस्तदर्थबुद्धिहेतुः इति निश्चिनोति इति कार्यार्थ एव व्युत्पत्तिरिति निर्बन्धो निर्निबन्धनः।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अतो वेदान्ताः परिनिष्पन्नं परं ब्रह्म, तद्-उपासनं चा[[पिरिमत|परिमित]]-फलं बोधयन्तीति तन्-निर्णय-फलो ब्रह्म-विचारः कर्तव्यः॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

The Vedānta-texts tell us about Brahman, which is an accomplished entity, and about meditation on Brahman as having an unlimited result, and hence it behoves us to undertake an enquiry into Brahman so as fully to ascertain its nature.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अतो वेदान्ताः परिनिष्पन्नं परं ब्रह्म, तदुपासनं चापिरिमतफलं बोधयन्तीति तन्निर्णयफलो ब्रह्मविचारः कर्तव्यः॥
</details>

## वेदस्य कार्यार्थत्वेऽपि ब्रह्मविचारारम्भसमर्थनम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

कार्यार्थत्वे ऽपि वेदस्य ब्रह्म-विचारः कर्तव्य एव।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

We further maintain that even on the supposition of the Veda relating only to things to be done, an enquiry into Brahman must be undertaken.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

कार्यार्थत्वेऽपि वेदस्य ब्रह्मविचारः कर्तव्य एव।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

कथम्; आत्मा वा अरे द्रष्टव्यश् श्रोतव्यो मन्तव्यो निदिध्यासितव्यः (बृ.४.४.५;६.५.६), सो ऽन्वेष्टव्यस् स विजिज्ञासितव्यः (छा.८.७.१), विज्ञाय प्रज्ञां कुर्वीत (बृ.६.४.२१), दहरो ऽस्मिन् अन्तराकाशस् तस्मिन् यद् अन्तस् तद् अन्वेष्टव्यं तद् वाव विजिज्ञा[[सतिव्यम्|सितव्यम्]] (छां.८.१.१), तत्रापि दह्रं गगनं विशोकस् तस्मिन् यद् [[अन्त |अन्तस् ]][[स्तदुपासतिव्यम्|तदुपा[[सतिव्यम्|सितव्यम्]]]] (तै.ना.१०.२३) इत्य्-आदिभिः प्रतिपन्नोपासन-विषय-कार्याधिकृत-फलत्वेन ब्रह्मविद् आप्नोति परम् (तै.आन.१.१) इत्य्-आदिभिर् ब्रह्म-[[प्राप्तिश्श्रूयत|प्राप्तिः श्रूयते]] इति ब्रह्म-स्वरूप-तद्-विशेषणानां दुःखासम्भिन्न-देश-विशेष-रूप-स्वर्गादिवत्, रात्रि-सत्र-प्रतिष्ठादिवत्, अपगोरण-शत-यातना-साध्य-साधन-भा[[वावच्च|भाववच् च]], कार्योपयोगितयैव सिद्धेः॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

For 'The Self is to be seen, to be heard, to be reflected on, to be meditated on' (Br̥. Up. II, 4, 5); 'He is to be searched out, him we must try to understand' (Cḥ. Up. VIII, 7, 1); 'Let a Brāhmaṇa having known him practise wisdom' (Br̥. Up. IV, 4, 21); 'What is within that small ether, that is to be sought for, that is to be understood' (Cḥ. Up. VIII, 1,1); 'What is in that small ether, that is to be meditated upon' (Mahānār. Up. X, 7)--these and similar texts enjoin a certain action, viz. meditation on Brahman, and when we then read 'He who knows Brahman attains the highest,' we understand that the attainment of Brahman is meant as a reward for him who is qualified for and enters on such meditation. Brahman itself and its attributes are thus established thereby only – that they subserve a certain action, viz. meditation. There are analogous instances in the Karmakāṇḍa of the Veda. When an arthavāda-passage describes the heavenly vorld as a place where there is no heat, no frost, no grief, &c., this is done merely with a view to those texts which enjoin certain sacrifices on those who are desirous of the heavenly world. Where another arthavāda says that 'those who perform certain sattra-sacrifices are firmly established,' such 'firm establishment' is referred to only because it is meant as the reward for those acting on the text which enjoins those sattras, 'Let him perform the rātri-sattras' (Pū. Mī. Sū. IV, 3, 17). And where a text says that a person threatening a Brāhmaṇa is to be punished with a fine of one hundred gold pieces, this statement is made merely with reference to the prohibitory passage, 'Let him not threaten a Brāhmaṇa'(Pū. Mī. Sū. III, 4, 17).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

कथम्; आत्मा वा अरे द्रष्टव्यश्श्रोतव्यो मन्तव्यो निदिध्यासितव्यः (बृ.४.४.५;६.५.६), सोऽन्वेष्टव्यस्सविजिज्ञासितव्यः (छा.८.७.१), विज्ञाय प्रज्ञां कुर्वीत (बृ.६.४.२१), दहरोऽस्मिन्नन्तराकाशस्तस्मिन्यदन्तस्तदन्वेष्टव्यं तद्वाव विजिज्ञासतिव्यम् (छां.८.१.१), तत्रापि दह्रं गगनं विशोकस्तस्मिन्यदन्त स्तदुपासतिव्यम् (तै.ना.१०.२३) इत्यादिभिः प्रतिपन्नोपासनविषयकार्याधिकृतफलत्वेन ब्रह्मविदाप्नोति परम् (तै.आन.१.१) इत्यादिभिर्ब्रह्म प्राप्तिश्श्रूयत इति ब्रह्मस्वरूपतद्विशेषणानां दुःखासम्भिन्नदेशविशेष-रूपस्वर्गादिवत्, रात्रिसत्रप्रतिष्ठादिवत्, अपगोरणशतयातनासाध्यसाधन-भावावच्च, कार्योपयोगितयैव सिद्धेः॥
</details>

अपूर्वखण्डनम्

## कार्यस्य दुर्निरूपतया कार्ये व्युत्पत्तिः न सिध्यति

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

गाम् आनय इत्य्-आदिष्व् अपि वाक्येषु न कार्यार्थे व्युत्पत्तिः; भवद्-अभिमत-कार्यस्य दुर्निरूपत्वात्।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

We, however, really object to the whole theory of the meaning of words depending on their connexion with 'things to be done,' since this is not even the case in imperative clauses such as 'bring the cow.' For you are quite unable to give a satisfactory definition of your 'thing to be done '(kārya).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

गामानय इत्यादिष्वपि वाक्येषु न कार्यार्थे व्युत्पत्तिः; भवदभिमतकार्यस्य दुर्निरूपत्वात्।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

कृति-भाव-भावि कृत्य्-उद्देश्यं हि भवतः कार्यम्।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

You understand by 'kārya' that which follows on the existence of action (kr̥ti) and is aimed at by action.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

कृतिभावभावि कृत्युद्देश्यं हि भवतः कार्यम्।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

कृत्य्-उद्देश्यत्वं च कृति-कर्मत्वम्।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

Now to be aimed at by action is to be the object(karman) of action,
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

कृत्युद्देश्यत्वं च कृतिकर्मत्वम्।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

कृति-कर्मत्वञ् च कृत्या प्राप्तुम् इष्टतमत्वम्।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

and to be the object of action is to be that which it is most desired to obtain by action (according to the grammarian's definition).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

कृतिकर्मत्वञ्च कृत्या प्राप्तुमिष्टतमत्वम्।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

इष्टतमं च सुखं वर्तमान-दुःखस्य तन्-निवृत्तिर् वा।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

But what one desires most to obtain is pleasure or the cessation of pain.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इष्टतमं च सुखं वर्तमानदुःखस्य तन्निवृत्तिर्वा।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तत्रेष्ट-सुखादिना पुरुषेण स्व-प्रयत्नाद् ऋते यदि तत्-सिद्धिः प्रतीता, ततः प्रयत्नेच्छुः प्रवर्तते पुरुष इति न क्वचिद् अपीच्छा-विषयस्य कृत्य्-अधीन-सिद्धित्वम् अन्तरेण कृत्य्-उद्देश्यत्वं नाम किञ्चिद् अप्य् उपलभ्यते।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

When a person desirous of some pleasure or cessation of pain is aware that his object is not to be accomplished without effort on his part, he resolves on effort and begins to act: in no case we observe an object of desire to be aimed at by action in any other sense than that of its accomplishment depending on activity.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तत्रेष्टसुखादिना पुरुषेण स्वप्रयत्नादृते यदि तत्सिद्धिः प्रतीता, ततः प्रयत्नेच्छुः प्रवर्तते पुरुष इति न क्वचिदपीच्छाविषयस्य कृत्यधीनसिद्धित्वमन्तरेण कृत्युद्देश्यत्वं नाम किञ्चिदप्युपलभ्यते।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

The prompting quality (prerakatva) also, which belongs to objects of desire, is nothing but the attribute of their accomplishment depending on activity; for it is this which moves to action.--
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

इच्छाविषयस्य प्रेरकत्वं च प्रयत्नाधीनसिद्धित्वमेव, तत एव प्रवृत्तेः।

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

<details><summary>English</summary>

Nor can it be said that 'to be aimed at by action' means to be that which is 'agreeable' (anukūla) to man; for it is pleasure only that is agreeable to man.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

न च पुरुषानुकूलत्वं कृत्युद्देश्यत्वम्, यतस्सुखमेव पुरुषानुकूलम्।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

The cessation of pain, on the other hand, is not what is 'agreeable' to man. The essential distinction between pleasure and pain is that the former is agreeable to man, and the latter disagreeable (pratikūla),
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

न च दुःखनिवृत्तेः पुरुषानुकूलत्वम्; पुरुषानुकूलं सुखं तत्प्रतिकूलं दुःखम् इति हि सुखदुःखयोस्स्वरूपविवेकः।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

and the cessation of pain is desired not because it is agreeable, but because pain is disagreeable:
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

दुःखस्य प्रतिकूलतया तन्निवृत्तिरिष्टा भवति; नानुकूलतया।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अनुकूल-प्रतिकूलान्वय-विरहे स्वरूपेणावस्थितिर् हि दुःख-निवृत्तिः।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

absence of pain means that a person is in his normal condition, affected neither with pain nor pleasure.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अनुकूलप्रतिकूलान्वयविरहे स्वरूपेणावस्थितिर्हि दुःखनिवृत्तिः।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अतस् सुख-व्यतिरिक्तस्य क्रियादेर् अनुकूलत्वं न सम्भवति।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

Apart from pleasure, action cannot possibly be agreeable,
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अतस्सुखव्यतिरिक्तस्य क्रियादेरनुकूलत्वं न सम्भवति।

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

<details><summary>English</summary>

nor does it become so by being subservient to pleasure; for its essential nature is pain.

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

न सुखार्थतया तस्याप्यनुकूलत्वम्, दुःखात्मकत्वात्तस्य।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

Nor, again, can we define that which is aimed at by action as that to which action is auxiliary or supplementary (śesha), while itself it holds the position of something principal to be subserved by other things (śeshin); for of the śesha and śeshin also no proper definition can be given.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

न च कृतिं प्रति शेषित्वं कृत्युद्देश्यत्वम्, भवत्पक्षे शेषित्वस्यानिरूपणात्॥
</details>

## शेषलक्षणम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

न च परोद्देश-प्रवृत्त-कृति-व्याप्त्य्-[[र्हात्वं|र्हत्वं]] शेषत्वम् इति तत्-प्रतिसम्बन्धी शेषीत्य् अवगम्यते, तथा सति कृतेर् अशेषत्वेन तां प्रति तत्-साध्यस्य शेषित्वाभावात्।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

It cannot be said that a śesha is that which is invariably accompanied by an activity proceeding with a view to something else, and that the correlate of such a śesha is the śeshin; for on this definition the action is not a śesha, and hence that which is to be effected by the action cannot be the correlative śeshin.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

न च परोद्देशप्रवृत्तकृतिव्याप्त्य[[र्हात्वं|र्हत्वं]] शेषत्वमिति तत्प्रतिसम्बन्धी शेषीत्यवगम्यते, तथा सति कृतेरशेषत्वेन तां प्रति तत्साध्यस्य शेषित्वाभावात्।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

न च परोद्देश-प्रवृत्त्य्-[[र्हातायाश्|र्हतायाश्]] शेषत्वेन परश् शेषी, उद्देश्यत्वस्यैव निरूप्यमाणत्वात्, प्रधानस्यापि भृत्योद्देश-प्रवृत्त्य्-[[र्हात्व|र्हत्व]]-दर्शनाच् च।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

And moreover a śeshin may not be defined as what is correlative to an action proceeding with a view to – i. e. aiming at – something else; for it is just this 'being aimed at' of which we require a definition, and moreover we observe that also the śeshin (or 'pradhāna') is capable of action proceeding with a view to the śesha, as when e.g. a master does something for – let us say, keeps or feeds – his servant.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

न च परोद्देशप्रवृत्त्य[[र्हातायाश्|र्हतायाश्]]शेषत्वेन परश्शेषी, उद्देश्यत्वस्यैव निरूप्यमाणत्वात्, प्रधानस्यापि भृत्योद्देशप्रवृत्त्य[[र्हात्व|र्हत्व]]दर्शनाच्च।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

प्रधानस् तु भृत्य-पोषणे ऽपि स्वोद्देशेन प्रवर्तत इति चेन् न, भृत्यो ऽपि हि प्रधान-पोषणे स्वोद्देशेनैव प्रवर्तते।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

This last criticism you must not attempt to ward off by maintaining that the master in keeping his servant acts with a view to himself (to his own advantage); for the servant in serving the master likewise acts with a view to himself.--
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

प्रधानस्तु भृत्यपोषणेऽपि स्वोद्देशेन प्रवर्तत इति चेन्न, भृत्योऽपि हि प्रधानपोषणे स्वोद्देशेनैव प्रवर्तते।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

कार्य-स्वरूपस्यैवानिरूपणात् कार्य-प्रतिसम्बन्धी शेषः, तत्-प्रतिसम्बन्धी शेषी इत्य् अप्य् असङ्गतम्॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

And as, further, we have no adequate definition of 'kārya,' it would be inappropriate to define śesha as that which is correlative to kārya, and śeshin as that which is correlative to śesha.--
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

कार्यस्वरूपस्यैवानिरूपणात् कार्यप्रतिसम्बन्धी शेषः, तत्प्रतिसम्बन्धी शेषी इत्यप्यसङ्गतम्॥
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

नापि कृति-प्रयोजनकत्वं कृत्य्-उद्देश्यत्वम्; पुरुषस्य कृत्य्-आरम्भ-प्रयोजनम् एव हि कृति-प्रयोजनम्।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

Nor, finally, may we define 'that which is aimed at by action' as that which is the final end (prayojana) of action; for by the final end of an action we could only understand the end for which the agent undertakes the action, and this end is no other than the desired object.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

नापि कृतिप्रयोजनकत्वं कृत्युद्देश्यत्वम्; पुरुषस्य कृत्यारम्भप्रयोजनमेव हि कृतिप्रयोजनम्।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स चेच्छा-विषयः।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

As thus 'what is aimed at by action' cannot be defined otherwise than what is desired,
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स चेच्छाविषयः।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

तस्माद् इष्टत्वातिरेकि-कृत्य्-उद्देश्यत्वानिरूपणात् कृति-साध्यता-कृति-प्रधानत्व-रूपं कार्यं दुर्निरूपम् एव॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

kārya cannot be defined as what is to be effected by action and stands to action in the relation of principal matter (pradhāna or śeshin).
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

तस्मादिष्टत्वातिरेकिकृत्युद्देश्यत्वानिरूपणात् कृतिसाध्यताकृतिप्रधानत्वरूपं कार्यं दुर्निरूपमेव॥
</details>

## अपूर्वे स्वतः इष्टत्व-कृतिसाध्यत्वरूपप्रयोजनत्वयोः निरासः

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

नियोगस्यापि साक्षाद् इ[[षि|ष्ट]]-विषय-भूत-सुख-दुःख-निवृत्तिभ्याम् अन्यत्वात् तत्-साधनतयैवेष्टत्वं कृति-साध्यत्वं च।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

(Let it then be said that the 'niyoga,' i.e. what is commonly called the apūrva – the supersensuous result of an action which later on produces the sensible result – constitutes the prayojana – the final purpose – of the action.--But) the apūrva also can, as it is something different from the direct objects of desire, viz. pleasure and the cessation of pain, be viewed only as a means of bringing about these direct objects, and as something itself to be effected by the action;
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

नियोगस्यापि साक्षादि[[षि|ष्ट]]विषयभूतसुखदुःखनिवृत्तिभ्यामन्यत्वात्तत्साधनतयैवेष्टत्वं कृतिसाध्यत्वं च।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अत एव हि तस्य क्रियातिरिक्तता; अन्यथा क्रियैव कार्यं स्यात्;
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

it is for this very reason that it is something different from the action, otherwise the action itself would be that which is effected by the action.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अत एव हि तस्य क्रियातिरिक्तता; अन्यथा क्रियैव कार्यं स्यात्;
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स्वर्ग-काम-पद-समभिव्याहारानुगुण्येन लिङादि-वाच्यं कार्यं स्वर्ग-साधनम् एवेति क्षणभङ्गि-कर्मातिरेकि स्थिरं स्वर्ग-साधनम् अपूर्वम् एव कार्यम् इति स्वर्ग-साधनतोल्लेखेनैव ह्य् अपूर्व-व्युत्पत्तिः।
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

The thing to be effected by the action-which is expressed by means of optative and imperative verbal forms such as yajeta, 'let him sacrifice '--is, in accordance with the fact of its being connected with words such as svargakāmaḥ, 'he who is desirous of heaven', understood to be the means of bringing about (the enjoyment of) the heavenly world; and as the (sacrificial) action itself is transitory, there is assumed an altogether 'new' or 'unprecedented' (apūrva) effect of it which (later on) is to bring about the enjoyment of heaven. This so-called 'apūrva' can therefore be understood only with regard to its capability of bringing about the heavenly world.
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

स्वर्गकामपदसमभिव्याहारानुगुण्येन लिङादिवाच्यं कार्यं स्वर्गसाधनमेवेति क्षणभङ्गिकर्मातिरेकि स्थिरं स्वर्गसाधनमपूर्वमेव कार्यमिति स्वर्गसाधनतोल्लेखेनैव ह्यपूर्वव्युत्पत्तिः।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अतः प्रथमम् अनन्यार्थतया प्रतिपन्नस्य कार्यस्यानन्यार्थत्व-निर्वहणायापूर्वम् एव पश्चात् स्वर्ग-साधनं भवतीत्य् उपहास्यम्, स्वर्ग-काम-पदान्वित-कार्याभिधायि-पदेन प्रथमम् अप्य् अनन्यार्थतानभिधानात्; सुख-दुःख-निवृत्ति-तत्-साधनेभ्यो ऽन्यस्य अनन्यार्थस्य कृति-साध्यता-प्रतीत्य्-अनुपपत्तेश् च ॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

Now it certainly is ludicrous to assert that the apūrva, which is assumed to the end of firmly establishing the independent character of the effect of the action first recognised as such (i.e. independent), later on becomes the means of realising the heavenly world; for as the word expressing the result of the action (yajta) appears in syntactical connexion with 'svargakāmaḥ' (desirous of heaven), it does not, from the very beginning, denote an independent object of action, and moreover it is impossible to recognise an independent result of action other than either pleasure or cessation of pain, or the means to bring about these two results.--
</details>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

अतः प्रथममनन्यार्थतया प्रतिपन्नस्य कार्यस्यानन्यार्थत्वनिर्वहणायापूर्वमेव पश्चात्स्वर्गसाधनं भवतीत्युपहास्यम्, स्वर्गकामपदान्वितकार्याभिधायिपदेन प्रथममप्यनन्यार्थतानभिधानात्; सुखदुःखनिवृत्तितत्साधनेभ्योऽन्यस्य अनन्यार्थस्य कृतिसाध्यताप्रतीत्यनुपपत्तेश्च ॥
</details>

<details><summary>English</summary>

It is pleasure alone which is agreeable!

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

सुखविशेषवन्नियोगापरपर्यायं विलक्षणं सुखान्तरमिति चेत्; किं तत्र प्रमाणमिति वक्तव्यम्।
</details>

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

स्वानुभवश् चेत्, न; विषय-विशेषानुभव-सुखवत् नियोगानुभव-सुखम् इदम् इति भवता ऽपि नानुभूयते।

<details><summary>English</summary>

You will, in the first place, admit yourself that you do not directly experience any pleasure springing from consciousness of your apūrva, which could in any way be compared to the pleasure caused by the consciousness of the objects of the senses.--

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

<details><summary>English</summary>

Nor, in the next place, Vedic texts; for those also enjoin action only as the means to bring about certain results such as the heavenly world.

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

<details><summary>मूलम्</summary>

नापि नित्यनैमित्तिकशास्त्रम्; तस्यापि तदभिधायित्वं स्वर्गकामवाक्यस्थापूर्वव्युत्पत्तिपूर्वकम् इत्युक्तरीत्या तेनापि सुखादिसाधनकार्याभिधानम् अवर्जनीयम्।

नियतैहिकफलस्य कर्मणोऽनुष्ठितस्य फलत्वेन तदानीमनुभूयमानान्नाद्यरोगतादि-व्यतिरेकेण नियोगरूपसुखानुभवानुपलब्धेश्च नियोगस्सुखमित्यत्र न किञ्चन प्रमाणमुपलभामहे।

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

अर्थवादादिष्व् अपि स्वर्गादि-सुख प्रकार-कीर्तनवत् नियोग-रूप-सुख-प्रकार-कीर्तनं भवतापि न दृष्ट-चरम्।
