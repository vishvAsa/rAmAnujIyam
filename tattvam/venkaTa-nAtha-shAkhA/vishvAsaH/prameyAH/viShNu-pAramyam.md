## Vedic proof
nArAyaNa is cause of universe as per veda.  
nArAyaNa is a visheShya, not visheShaNa as per vyAkaraNa / grammar, unlike rudra, viShNu, indra etc.  
nArAyaNa is said to be husband of lakShmI in shruti - ह्रीश् च ते लक्ष्मीश् च पत्न्यौ। 
Hence proved.

Besides this, nArAyaNa is famous as being synonymous to viShNu husband of lakShmI.  


## गोपथ-ब्राह्मणम्
> ओङ्कारस्य चोत्पत्तिः .... 
पुंसो वत्सः रुद्रो देवता

Gopatha Brāhmaṇa (1.1.25) 

Only says rudra, son of puruSha is the deity responsible for generation of omkAra.  
"प्रजापतिर्वै रुद्रं यज्ञान्निरभजत्" in the same brAhmaNa makes it clear rudra is inferior to prajApati.

Hence 

> establishes Supreme Rudra beyond the Trimūrti, as the adhiṣṭhātṛ of Praṇava (ॐ)

is false.

## शिवसङ्कल्पोपनिषत्

> परात् परतरो ब्रह्मा तत्परात् परतो हरिः ।  
तत्परात् परतो ऽधीशस् तन्मे मनः शिवसङ्कल्पमस्तु ॥ १८॥

Here, अधीशः refers to the Atma described in 

> योऽसौ सर्वेषु वेदेषु पठ्यते ह्यज इश्वरः ।  
अकायो निर्गुणो ह्यात्मा तन्मे मनः शिवसङ्कल्पमस्तु ॥

while हरि refers to a particular embodiment of the same.
