## ०१ कण् सोर वॆम् कुरुदि वन्दु इऴिय

कण् सोर वॆम् कुरुदि वन्दु इऴिय  
वॆम् तऴल्बोल् कून्दलाळै  
मण् सेर मुलै उण्ड मा मदलाय्  
वाऩवर् तम् कोवे ऎऩ्ऱु  
विण् सेरुम् इळन् दिङ्गळ् अगडु उरिञ्जु  
मणि माडम् मल्गु सॆल्वत्  
तण् सेऱै ऎम् पॆरुमाऩ् ताळ् तॊऴुवार्  
काण्मिऩ् ऎऩ् तलैमेलारे (७।४।१)

<details><summary>अर्थः (UV) - DP_१५७८</summary>

क्रूरमाऩ कण्गळ् सुऴलवुम् पिऩ् तळरवुम् उष्णमाऩ रत्तम् पॆरुगुम्बडि नॆरुप्पैप् पोऩ्ऱ कून्दलैयुडैय पूदऩै माण्डुबोगुम्बडि पालै उऱिञ्जियुण्ड इळम्बिळ्ळैये! तेवर्गळ् तलैवऩे! ऎऩ्ऱु आगायत्तिल् इरुक्कुम् इळम् सन्दिरऩिऩ् कीऴ्वयिऱ्‌ऱैत् तॊडुमळवु रत्ऩ मयमाऩ माळिगैगळ् निऱैन्द सॆल्वम् मिक्क कुळिर्न्द तिरुच्चेऱैयिलिरुक्कुम् ऎम् पॆरुमाऩिऩ् पादङ्गळैप् पऱ्‌ऱुबवर्गळ् ऎऩ् तलैमेल् पादङ्गळै वैत्तु उलावुवदै पारुङ्गळ्
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_१५७८</summary>

Your devotees praise you saying,
      “O king of the gods!
      When you were a baby you drank poisonous milk
      from the breasts of Putanā with fire-like red hair,
      and her blood flowed out swiftly,
      her eyes became tired and she fell to the ground.
      You are a strong child,
      and you are the king of the gods in the sky.”
      You are the god of rich Thiruthaṇcheṛai
      surrounded with diamond-studded palaces
      that touch the crescent moon in the sky.
      See, folding my hands on my head, I worship
      those devotees who worship the feet of you, our god.
      .
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_१५७९</summary>

On his chest he keeps Lakshmi with long and sharp eyes like arrows.
      He is the dancer who threw the Asuran when he came as a calf
      at the Asuran who had taken the form of a vilām fruit
      and killed them both.
      If the devotees worship the feet of my divine god,
      in the temple of Thiruthaṇcheṛai
      surrounded with cool groves where bees swarm,
      they will stay in my heart always.
</details>

      of the flowers and sing,

      came like a lightning bolt before you,
      you cut off her nose and ears with your divine hands.”
      I worship the feet of those virtuous devotees
      who sprinkle water and flowers on the god’s feet
      and shine brighter than the gods in the sky.

अऩ्ऩप्पऱवैगळ् वयल्गळिल् पॆडैयोडु अऴगिय पूक्कळणिन्द करुङ्गून्दलैयुडैय इळम् पॆण्गळैप्पोल नडन्दु तॆऩ्ऩैमरत्तिऩुडैय निऴलिल् कण् तुयिलुम् कुळिर्न्द तिरुच्चेऱै पॆरुमाऩैक् कुऱित्तु ओडिवरुम् कुदिरैगळ् पूट्टिऩ तेरैयुडैयराऩ परगालऩ् ऎऩ्ऩुम् तिरुमङ्गैयाऴ्वार् अरुळिच् चॆय्द पासुरङ्गळैक् कॊण्डु ओ पक्तर्गळे! तूय अऴगिय पॊऩ् पोऩ्ऱ पासुरङ्गळै तिरुवडिगळिऩ् मेल् उङ्गळ् कूप्पिऩ कैयाल् अञ्जलि सॆय्दु वणङ्गि सूट्टुङ्गळ्

<details><summary>Hart - DP_१५८७</summary>

The poet Kaliyan, Yama to his enemies,
      who drives swift chariots yoked with horses,
      composed ten musical pāsurams on the god
      resting on Adisesha on the water in cool Thiruthaṇcheṛai
      where swans beneath the shadows of a mango trees
      are with their mates that walk like women
      wearing flowers in their dark hair.
      O devotees! Fold your hands together
      and worship his pure golden feet.
----------
