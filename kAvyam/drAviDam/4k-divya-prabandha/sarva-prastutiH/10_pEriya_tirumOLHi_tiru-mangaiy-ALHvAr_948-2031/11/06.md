तॊण्डर्गळे! मै पोऩ्ऱ करुत्त कडलिडत्तिल् उलगम् पिरळय कालत्तिल् ऒऩ्ऱुम् इल्लामल् तेवर्गळुम् नामुम् ऎल्लाप् पॊरुळ्गळुम् नॆय्यिट्टु विळङ्गुम् सक्करत्तैयुडैय ऎम्बॆरुमाऩिऩ् तिरुवयिऱ्‌ऱिल् पलगालम् वैत्तु कात्त ऎम्बॆरुमाऩै अऱियविल्लैये विरोदिगळिऩ् तॆय्वत्तै ऎऩ्ऩ उबगारम् सॆय्दार्गळ् ऎऩ्ऱु तुदिक्किऩ्ऱीर्गळ् ऎम्बॆरुमाऩ् सॆय्दनऩ्ऱियै मऱक्कादीर्गळ् अन्द पॆरुमाऩैये तुदित्तु वणङ्गुङ्गळ्
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_२००२</summary>

At the end of the eon,
      he swallowed all the gods’ worlds
      and the people of the seven worlds
      surrounded with dark oceans
      and he kept them in his divine stomach for a long time.
      Think about it, O devotees.
      You worship the gods of other religions who are our enemies.
      What good did they do for us? Don’t be ungrateful.
      Worship only our lord in the sky
      who carries a discus smeared with oil.
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_२००३</summary>

When the abundant flood came at the end of the eon
      and rose to the sky, the lord with his strong hands
      took all oceans with their water and swallowed them all.
      Everyone knows this.

      who was not swallowed and spit out by our god?
      O devotees, don’t you know that the world
      is here through his grace?
</details>

## ०४ पऩिप् परवैत् तिरै तदुम्बप् पार् ऎल्लाम्

एऴिऩैयुम् ऊऴिल् वाङ्गि
