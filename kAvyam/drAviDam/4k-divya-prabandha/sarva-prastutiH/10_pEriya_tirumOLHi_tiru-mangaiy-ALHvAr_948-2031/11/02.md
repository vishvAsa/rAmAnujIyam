कुऩ्ऱम् ऎडुत्तु

## ०१ कुऩ्ऱम् ऎडुत्तु मऴै तडुत्तु इळैयारॊडुम्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_१९६२ - ०१</summary>

कुऩ्ऱम् ऎडुत्तु मऴै तडुत्तु इळैयारॊडुम्  
मऩ्ऱिल् कुरवै पिणैन्द माल् ऎऩ्ऩै माल्सॆय्दाऩ्  
मुऩ्ऱिल् तऩि निऩ्ऱ पॆण्णैमेल् किडन्दु ईर्गिऩ्ऱ  
अऩ्ऱिलिऩ् कूट्टैप् पिरिक्कगिऱ्‌पवर् आर्गॊलो? (११।२।१)

</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_१९६२</summary>

She says,
      “The lord who carried Govardhana mountain as an umbrella
      and blocked the storm, saving the cows and the cowherds,
      and who danced the Kuravai dance with young girls in the mandram
      has enchanted me.
      The andril bird on the palm tree in the front yard coos and hurts me.
      Who can remove its nest so it will not coo and call its absent mate?”
</details>

## ०२ पूङ् गुरुन्दु ऒसित्तु आऩै काय्न्दु अरि माच् चॆगुत्तु

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_१९६३ - ०२</summary>

पूङ् गुरुन्दु ऒसित्तु आऩै काय्न्दु अरि माच् चॆगुत्तु  
आङ्गु वेऴत्तिऩ् कॊम्बु कॊण्डु वऩ् पेय् मुलै  
वाङ्गि उण्ड अव् वायऩ् निऱ्‌क इव् आयऩ् वाय्  
एङ्गु वेय्ङ्गुऴल् ऎऩ्ऩोडु आडुम् इळमैये (११।२।२)

<details><summary>Hart - DP_१९६३</summary>

She says,
      “The heroic Kaṇṇan who shattered the blooming Kurundam trees,
      and broke the tusks of the elephant Kuvalayābeeḍam
      fought with the Asuran who came as a horse,
      took the form of a man-lion and killed Hiraṇyan,
      and drank milk from the devil Putana and killed her.
      When Kaṇṇan is with me what can the music
      of the bamboo flute of a cowherd do to my youth?”
</details>

      and the bells of the strong bulls returning from the fields give me pain.”
</details>
