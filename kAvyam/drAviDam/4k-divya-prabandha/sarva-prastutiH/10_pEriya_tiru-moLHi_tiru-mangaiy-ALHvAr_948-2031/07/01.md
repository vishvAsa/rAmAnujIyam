## ०१ कऱवा मड नागु तऩ् कऩ्ऱु उळ्ळिऩाल् पोल्

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_१५४८ - ०१</summary>

कऱवा मड नागु तऩ् कऩ्ऱु उळ्ळिऩाल् पोल्  
मऱवादु अडियेऩ् उऩ्ऩैये अऴैक्किऩ्ऱेऩ्  
नऱवु आर् पॊऴिल् सूऴ् नऱैयूर् निऩ्ऱ नम्बि  
पिऱवामै ऎऩैप् पणि ऎन्दै पिराऩे (७।१।१)

<details><summary>अर्थः (UV) - DP_१५४८</summary>

तेऩ्मिक्क सोलैगळाल् सूऴ्न्द तिरुनऱैयूरिल् इरुक्कुम् पॆरुमाऩे! पाल्सुरवाद इळम्बसु अदऩ् कऩ्ऱै निऩैत्तु कत्तुमाप्पोल् अडियेऩ् मऱवामल् उऩ्ऩैये अऴैक्किऩ्ऱेऩ् ऎऩ् तन्दैयाऩ पॆरुमाऩे! ऎऩ्ऩै पिऱवादबडि सॆय्दरुळ्वाय्
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_१५४८</summary>

Like a young calf that craves its mother’s milk
      I call you unceasingly.
      You, our Nambi, stay in Naṛaiyur
      surrounded with groves dripping with honey.
      Give me your grace so I will not be born again,
      my father and my god.
</details>

## ०२ वऱ्‌ऱा मुदुनीरॊडु माल् वरै एऴुम्

तुऱ्‌ऱु आग मुऩ् तुऱ्‌ऱिय तॊल् पुगऴोऩे

<details><summary>अर्थः (UV) - DP_१५४९</summary>

वऱ्‌ऱाद कडल्गळैयुम् पॆरिय एऴु मलैगळैयुम् मुऩ्बॊरुसमयम् ऒरु कबळमाग वारि विऴुङ्गिय कीर्त्तियैयुडैयवऩे! ऎऩ् तन्दैये! उऩक्के तासऩाऩ अडियेऩ् उऩ्ऩैये अऴैक्कुम् इप्पॆरुम् पेऱ्‌ऱैप् पॆऱ्‌ऱेऩ् अरुळ् तन्दिडवेण्डुम्

<details><summary>Hart - DP_१५४९</summary>

Ancient and famous,
      you swallowed all the oceans that never dry
      and the seven hills.
      I have no one—I am your slave.
      I call you, I come to you.
      Give me your grace, my father and lord.
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_१५५०</summary>

You gave me your grace
      and I will not give it away to anyone else.
      I drink your grace and relish it—it is never enough for me.
      You have the dark color of the ocean
      and are like a mountain, O god of Thirukkoṭṭiyur.
      You are happy to have me as your devotee
      and I, your slave, have received you with joy.
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_१५५१</summary>

      “O faultless one, you split open the beak of the bird,”

      you are sweet as sugarcane, you are generous,
      you rest on the ocean in Thirumallai.
      How could I forget you?

<details><summary>अर्थः (UV) - DP_१५५७</summary>

तॊण्डर्गळे! वण्डुगळ् निऱैन्द सोलैगळाल् सूऴन्द तिरुनऱैयूरिलिरुक्कुम् पॆरुमाऩुक्कु ऎऩ्ऱुम् अडिमैबूण्डु तिरुमङ्गैयाऴ्वार् अरुळिच् चॆय्द तमिऴ्प् पासुरङ्गळ् इवै पत्तुम् पाडुङ्गळ् अप्पडि निऩ्ऱु पाडि आड उङ्गळुक्कु तुयरे इल्लै परमबदम् निच्चयम्
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_१५५७</summary>

Kaliyan, the devotee of the god,
      composed a garland of musical Tamil pāsurams
      on him, the god of Thirunaṛaiyur
      surrounded by groves swarming with bees.
      O devotees, if you sing these pāsurams and dance,
      you will go to heaven and your troubles will disappear.
-----------
</details>
