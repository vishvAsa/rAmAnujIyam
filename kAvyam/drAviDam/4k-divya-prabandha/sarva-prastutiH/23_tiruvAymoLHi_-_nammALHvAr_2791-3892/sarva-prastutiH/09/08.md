## ०१ अरुक्कुम्विनैयायिन आगत्तवनै

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_३७५० - ०१</summary>

अरुक्कुम्विनैयायिन आगत्तवनै  
निरुत्तुम्मनत्तॊन्रिय शिन्दैयिनार्क्कु  
वॆरित्तण्मलर्शोलैगळ्चूळ् तिरुनावाय्  
कुरुक्कुं वगैयुण्डुगॊलो? कॊडियेर्के.

<details><summary>Hart - DP_३७५०</summary>

If devotees keep the lord in their minds
      and think only of him their karma will go away.
      When will the day come when I, so unworthy,
      can approach Thirunavay,
      surrounded by fragrant cool blooming groves.
</details>

## ०२ कॊडियेरिडै क्कोगनगत्तवळ्केळ्वन्

नॆडियानुरै शोलैगळ्शूळ् तिरुनावाय्  
अडियेन् अणुगप्पॆरुनाळ् ऎवैगॊलो?

<details><summary>Hart - DP_३७५१</summary>

      her waist as thin as a vine,
      and the beloved of lovely Nappinnai
      with large sharp spear-like eyes.
      When will I, his slave, approach Thirunāvāy
      where the lord Nediyān stays, surrounded by groves?
</details>
