## ०१ पॊरुळाल् अमर् उलगम् पुक्कु इयलल् आगादु

<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_२२२२ - ०१</summary>

अरुळाल् अऱम् अरुळुम् अऩ्ऱे ? अरुळाले  
मा मऱैयोर्क्कु ईन्द मणिवण्णऩ् पादमे  
नी मऱवेल् नॆञ्जे! निऩै (१४।५।१)

<details><summary>Hart - DP_२२२२</summary>

No one can enter the world of the gods just by wealth.
      The only way to attain moksha is by his grace.
      O heart, don't forget his sapphire-colored feet
      that give moksha to divine sages.
      Always meditate on his names.
</details>

## ०२ निऩैप्पऩ् तिरुमालै नीण्ड तोळ् काण

<details><summary>Hart - DP_२२२३</summary>

I think of Thirumāl always.
      If devotees worship and meditate on the wide arms of the lord,
      they will not have any future births.
      Just by worshiping him
      the sages get all the happiness
      that those receive who were born
      to enjoy family life in this world.
</details>
