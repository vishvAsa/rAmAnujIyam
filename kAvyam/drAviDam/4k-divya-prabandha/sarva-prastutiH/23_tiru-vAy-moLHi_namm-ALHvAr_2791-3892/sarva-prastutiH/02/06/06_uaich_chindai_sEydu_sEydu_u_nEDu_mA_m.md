<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_२९६१ - ०६</summary>

उऩ्ऩैच् चिन्दै सॆय्दु सॆय्दु उऩ् नॆडु मा मॊऴि इसै पाडि आडि ऎऩ्  
मुऩ्ऩैत् तीविऩैगळ् मुऴु वेर् अरिन्दऩऩ् याऩ्  
उऩ्ऩैच् चिन्दैयिऩाल् इगऴ्न्द इरणियऩ् अगल् मार्वम् कीण्ड ऎऩ्  
मुऩ्ऩैक् कोळरिये मुडियाददु ऎऩ् ऎऩक्के?। २।६।६
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_२९६१</summary>

I thought and thought of you  
and praised you in songs and danced  
and all the bad karma of my former births went away.  
You took the form of a man-lion  
and split open the chest of Hiraṇyan when he disgraced you.  
I am your slave. There is nothing that I cannot do.  
</details>
