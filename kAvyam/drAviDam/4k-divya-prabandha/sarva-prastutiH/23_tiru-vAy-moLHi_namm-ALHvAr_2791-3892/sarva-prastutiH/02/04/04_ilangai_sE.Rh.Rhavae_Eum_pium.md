<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_२९३७ - ०४</summary>

इलङ्गै सॆऱ्‌ऱवऩे ऎऩ्ऩुम् पिऩ्ऩुम्  
वलम् कॊळ् पुळ् उयर्त्ताय् ऎऩ्ऩुम् उळ्ळम्  
मलङ्ग वॆव् उयिर्क्कुम् कण्णीर् मिगक्  
कलङ्गिक् कैदॊऴुम् निऩ्ऱु इवळे। २।४।४
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_२९३७</summary>

Her mother says,  
“She says ‘You destroyed Lanka  
and raised your eagle banner in victory.’  
She sheds many tears, her mind is confused  
and her sighs are like burning fire.  
She folds her hands and worships you.”  
</details>
