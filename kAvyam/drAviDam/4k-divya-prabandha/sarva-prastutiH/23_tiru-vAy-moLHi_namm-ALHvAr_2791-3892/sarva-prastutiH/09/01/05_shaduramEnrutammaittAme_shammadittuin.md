<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_३६७७ - ०५</summary>

शदुरमॆन्रुतम्मैत्तामे शम्मदित्तुइन्मॊळियार्  
मदुरपोगं तुऱ्ऱवरे वैगिमऱ्ऱॊन्रुरुवर्  
अदिर्गॊळ् शॆय्गैयशुरर्मङ्ग वडमदुरैप्पिरन्दार्कु  
ऎदिर्गॊळाळाय् उय्यलल्लाल् इल्लैकण्डीरिन्बमे.

<details><summary>Hart - DP_३६७७</summary>

Those who think that a happy life  
is to live with women whose words are sweet  
and to experience honey-like pleasure with them  
will be chased away by those women when they become poor.  
There is no way to survive unless you becomes a devotee of him  
who was born in northern Mathura,  
and fought and destroyed the cruel Asurans.  
</details>
