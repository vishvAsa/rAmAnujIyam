Hart - DP_३७५६

As a dwarf you took the land from king Mahābali,
and you destroyed the Asurans when the gods fought them.
O Thirumāl, Nambi Narayaṇan of Thirunāvāy,
come to me, give me your grace and say, “This is my devotee!”
