<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_३५४५ - ०५</summary>

नेर्पडयान् शॊल्लुम् नीर्मैयिलामैयिल्  
एर्विला ऎन्नैत्तन्नाक्कि ऎन्नाल्तन्नै  
पार्परविन्कवि पाडुम्परमरे.

<details><summary>Hart - DP_३५४५</summary>

The highest lord understood that I,  
a low one, have no ability to compose sweet poems.  
He joined me with himself, and composed wonderful poems  
that are praised by the whole world.  
</details>
