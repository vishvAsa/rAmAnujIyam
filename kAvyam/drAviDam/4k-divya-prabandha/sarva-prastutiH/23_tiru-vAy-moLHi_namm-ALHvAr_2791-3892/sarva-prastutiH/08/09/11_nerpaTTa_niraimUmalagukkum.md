<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_३६६१ - ११</summary>

नेर्पट्ट निरैमूमलगुक्कुम् नायगन्तन्नडिमै  
नेर्पट्ट तॊण्डर्तॊण्डर्तॊण्डर् तॊण्डन् शटगोपन् शॊल्  
नेर्पट्ट तमिळ्मालै आयिरत्तुळ्ळिवैपत्तुम्  
नेर्पट्टारवर् नेर्पट्टार् नॆटुमार्कडिमैशॆय्यवे.
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_३६६१</summary>

Saḍagopan, the devotee of the devotees  
of the devotees of the devotees,  
became the slave of the lord of the marvelous three worlds  
and composed a garland of a thousand Tamil pāsurams.  
If devotees learn and recite these ten pāsurams  
they will have the fortune of becoming the slaves of Neḍumal.  
</details>

<details><summary>प्रतिपदार्थः (UV) - DP_३६६१</summary>
