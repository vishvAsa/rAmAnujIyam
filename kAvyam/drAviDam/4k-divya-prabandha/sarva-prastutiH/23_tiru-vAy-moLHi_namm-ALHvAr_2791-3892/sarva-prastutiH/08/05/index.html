I call you saying, “Come, I want to see you!” and my eyes grow tired and my mouth dry. I am ashamed calling and calling you— I suffer to see you. Won’t you feel sorry for me and come? Come to me shining like a large dark diamond mountain behind which a dark sun rises at dawn, O father with a shining crown on your dark hair.
