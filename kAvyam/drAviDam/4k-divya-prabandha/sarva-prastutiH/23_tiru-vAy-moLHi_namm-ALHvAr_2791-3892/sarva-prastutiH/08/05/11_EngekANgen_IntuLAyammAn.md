<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_३६१७ - ११</summary>

ऎङ्गेकाण्गेन्\! ईन्तुळायम्मान् तन्नैयान्? ऎन्रॆन्रु  
अङ्गेताळ्न्दशॊर्कळाल् अन्दण्कुरुगूर्च्चडगोपन्  
शॆङ्गेळ् शॊन्नवायिरत्तुळ् इवैयुम्पत्तुम् वल्लार्गळ्  
इङ्गेकाण इप्पिरप्पेमगिळ्वर् ऎल्लियुङ्कालैये.
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_३६१७</summary>

Saḍagopan of cool flourishing Thirukuruhur  
composed a thousand pāsurams  
with beautiful music and divine words  
in which a devotee asks the god,  
“Where can I see my father who wears a fresh thulasi garland?”  
If devotees learn and recite these ten pāsurams  
they will be happy night and day in this birth itself  
because they know the nature of god.  
</details>
