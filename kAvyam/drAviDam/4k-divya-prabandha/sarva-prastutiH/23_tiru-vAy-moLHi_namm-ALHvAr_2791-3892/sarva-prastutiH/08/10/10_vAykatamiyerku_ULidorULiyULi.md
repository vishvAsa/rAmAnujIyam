<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_३६७१ - १०</summary>

वाय्कतमियेर्कु ऊळिदोरूळियूळि माकायाम्  
पूक्कॊळ्मेनि नान्गुतोळ्पॊन्नाळिक्कै ऎन्नम्मान्  
नीक्कमिल्लावडियार्तम् अडियारडियारडियारॆं  
कोक्कळ् अवर्क्केकुडिगळाय् शॆल्लुं नल्लकोळ्पाडे.

<details><summary>Hart - DP_३६७१</summary>

My father with four arms and a dark body like a flower  
carries a golden discus in his hand and abides eon after eon.  
My only wish is to be the slave  
of the devotees of the devotees of the devotees of him.  
They are my lords, for they never leave our god.  
</details>
