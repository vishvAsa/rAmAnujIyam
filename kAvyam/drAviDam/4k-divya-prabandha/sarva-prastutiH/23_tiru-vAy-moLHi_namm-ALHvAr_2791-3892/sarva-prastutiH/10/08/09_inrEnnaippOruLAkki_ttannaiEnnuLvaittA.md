<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_३८६८ - ०९</summary>

इन्रॆन्नैप्पॊरुळाक्कि त्तन्नैऎन्नुळ्वैत्तान्  
अन्रु ऎन्नैप्पुरम्पोग प्पुणर्त्तदु ऎन् शॆय्वान्  
कुन्रॆन्रत्तिगळ्माडङ्गळ्शूळ् तिरुप्पेरान्  
ऒन्रु ऎनक्करुळ्शॆय्य उणर्त्तलुऱ्ऱेने.
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_३८६८</summary>

Entering my heart where I keep him,  
he has made me remarkable today.  
Why did he make me wander about  
involved in the pleasures of five senses?  
What did he gain by that?  
The lord of Thiruperur surrounded by shining hills  
that are like palaces gave me his grace  
and I understood why I was hurt by my senses.  
</details>
