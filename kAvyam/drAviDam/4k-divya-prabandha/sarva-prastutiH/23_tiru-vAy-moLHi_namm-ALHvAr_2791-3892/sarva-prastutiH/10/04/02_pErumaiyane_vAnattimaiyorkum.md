<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_३८१७ - ०२</summary>

पॆरुमैयने वानत्तिमैयोर्कुं काण्डर्  
करुमैयने आगत्तणैयादार्क्कॆन्रुम्  
तिरुमॆय्युरैगिन्र शॆङ्गण्माल्नाळुम्  
इरुमैविनैकडिन्दु इङ्गॆन्नैयाळ्गिन्राने.
</details>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_३८१७</summary>

He is praised by all the gods in the sky  
yet it is impossible for them to see him.  
If devotees do not embrace him in their hearts  
they will not be able to see the beautiful lotus-eyed Maal  
and lovely Lakshmi who stays on his chest as he embraces her.  
He destroyed my good and bad karma and rules me here.  
</details>
