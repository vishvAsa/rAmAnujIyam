<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_३८२४ - ०९</summary>

<details><summary>Hart - DP_३८२४</summary>

As soon as I found the lotus feet of the lord  
and saw him all my karma was destroyed.  
I worship him and live serving others always  
as the highest lord has commanded us to do  
from ancient times.  
</details>
