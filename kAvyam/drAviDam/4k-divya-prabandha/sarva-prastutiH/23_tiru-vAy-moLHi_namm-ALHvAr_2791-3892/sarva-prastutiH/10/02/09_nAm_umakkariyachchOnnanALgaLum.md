<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - DP_३८०२ - ०९</summary>

नाम् उमक्करियच्चॊन्ननाळ्गळुम् नणियवान  
शेमम्नन्गुडैत्तुक्कण्डीर् शॆरिपॊळिल् अनन्तपुरम्  
तूमनल्विरैमलर्गळ् तुवळरवाय्न्दुकॊण्डु  
वामननडिक्कॆन्रेत्त माय्न्दरुं विनैगळ्तामे.

<details><summary>Hart - DP_३८०२</summary>

Gather fresh fragrant flowers to sprinkle  
on the feet of our god Vamanan  
and put out incense and worship his feet.  
If you go to Anandapuram  
surrounded by thick blooming groves  
and worship the lord,  
all your karma will go away.  
I have been saying this for a long time—  
there is only a little time left.  
</details>

<details><summary>प्रतिपदार्थः (UV) - DP_३८०२</summary>

<details><summary>गरणि-विस्तारः - DP_३८०२ - ०९</summary>
