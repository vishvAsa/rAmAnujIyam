## Lacunum
In 1895 brits framed a case on AS36 that he was aiding anti british forces (early independence movement) and put AS36 on house arrest in Thiruvallur. AS36 escaped with some vigrahas to pAdur where he was later put in brndavana.

AS37 took sanyasa from Denkanikottai Swamy and became jiyar and told the collector that I am not responsible for whatever AS36 did and got the matha out of house-arrest in Thiruvallur. 

After AS37 died, due to both internal politics and british interference, mutt did not have a pontiff from 1900 to 1905. In 1905 a new jiyar was allowed to become a jiyar after agreeing to appointment of agent etc.

In 1905 Ahobilam, Thiruvallur, kumbhakonam, Srirangam Agent appointment was mandated. Currently no mandate still the practice continues except kumbhakonam.

## tiruvallUr context
The reason for framing this case was to takeover administration of Thiruvallur Temple. Even then it was under the matha but british government wanted to takeover the temple and bring the temple under its legal framework of appointing trustees, external audits, budget verifications etc..

Till 2000 Thiruvallur temple was a tussle between matha and government. Current scheme came in 2000-2001 only.
