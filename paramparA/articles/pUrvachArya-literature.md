[SrIman nArAyaNan](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/), for the upliftment of the samsAris (bound souls), out of his causeless mercy, reveals the SAsthram (vedam) to brahmA during Srushti (creation). vedam is the ultimate pramANam for vaidhikas. A pramAthA (AchAryan) can determine the [pramEyam (emperumAn)](http://ponnadi.blogspot.com/2013/03/tattva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god.html) only through [pramANam (SAsthram)](https://srivaishnavagranthams.wordpress.com/). 

## veda
Just like emperumAn has akila hEya prathyanIkathvam (opposite to all bad qualities) and kalyANaikathAnathvam (abode of all auspicious qualities) which differentiate emperumAn from every other entity, vedam has the following important qualities (that differentiate it from other pramANams):

- apaurushEyathvam – not created by any individual (at the outset of every creation, emperumAn teaches vedam to brahmA who in turn propagates the same). Hence errors of individual’s sense-perception etc., are absent.

- nithyam – it is eternal – with no beginning and no end – it is revealed time and again by emperumAn who is fully cognizant of the contents of vedam.

- svatha prAmANyathvam – all veda vAkyams are self-sufficient, i.e., we dont need anything more to prove the validity of the same.

While vedam is just one vast body of scripture, veda vyAsa, considering the limited capacity of human intelligence in the future, divided the vedam into rig, yajur, sAma and atharva vedams.

vedAntam is the essence of vedam. vedAntam is the collection of upanishadhs which talks about the intricate details about emperumAn. upanishadhs are the works of great sages which analyse brahmam from different aspects. While vedam talks about the process of worship, vedAntam talks about bhagavAn who is the object of worship. There are many upanishadhs, but the following are considered as the most important:

- aitharEya

- bruhadhAraNyaka

- chAndhOgya

- isha


- kEna

- kathA

- kaushIdhiki

- mahA nArAyaNa

- mAndukya

- muNdaka

- prashNa

- subhAla

- svEthasvathAra

- thaiththiriya

brahma sUthram that is compiled by sage veda vyAsa is also considered to be part of vedAntam since it is the essence of upanishadhs.

## upabRMhaNam
Since, vedam is anantham (endless – huge) and vedAntam is very complicated and the capacity of human intelligence is limited (which can lead to misunderstandings and misinterpretations), we have to understand vedam/vedAntam through smruthi, ithihAsam and purANams.

- smruthi is the collection of dharma SAsthrams which are compiled by great sages like manu, vishNu hAritha, yAgyavalkya, etc.

- ithihAsams are the collection of SrI rAmAyaNam and mahAbhArtham – the two great epics. SrI rAmAyaNam is considered as the sharaNAgathi SAsthram and mAhAbhAratham is considered as panchamO vedam (fifth vedam after the rig, yajur, sAma and atharva vedams).

- purANams are the collection of 18 main purANams (brahma purANam, padhma purANam, vishNu purANam, etc) and many upa (minor) purANams which are compiled by brahmA. Of these 18 purANams, brahmA himself declares that when he is in the mode of sathvam (goodness) he glorifies vishNu, in the mode of rajas (passion) he glorifies himself and in the mode of thamas (ignorance) he glorifies Siva, agni, etc.

## ALvArs
Even with the existence of all of these, instead of gaining true knowledge through SAsthram and looking out for the true goal, samsAris were still engaged in materialistic aspirations. [emperumAn](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) then personally descended as many avathArams, only to see many disrespecting him and even fighting with him. Realizing that he should prepare a jIvAthmA to help the samsAris (like a deer is used to capture another deer by a hunter), he picked a select few jIvAthmAs and gave them blemishless divine knowledge. These jIvAthmAs became to be famously known as [AzhwArs](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/azhwars/) (ones who are immersed in bhagavath anubhavam). They are headed by [nammAzhwA](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/)r (prapanna kula kUtasthar/vaishNava kulapathi) and are said to be 10 in number – [poigai AzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/poigai-azhwar/), [bhUthaththAzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/bhuthaththazhwar/), [pEyAzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/peyazhwar/), [thirumazhisai AzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/thirumazhisai-azhwar/), [nammAzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/), [kulaSEkara AzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/kulasekarazhwar/), [periyAzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periyazhwar/), [thoNdaradippodi AzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/thondaradippodi-azhwar/), [thiruppANAzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/thiruppanazhwar/) and [thirumangai AzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/thirumangai-azhwar/). [madhurakavi AzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/madhurakavi-azhwar/) (disciple of [nammAzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/)) and [ANdAL](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/andal/) (daughter of [periyAzhwAr](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periyazhwar/)) are also added into AzhwArs goshti at times. AzhwArs who were fully blessed by emperumAn preached the divine knowledge to many. Still, since they were immersed in bhagavath anubhavam their focus was on mangaLAsAsanam to emperumAn.

[emperumAn](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/), wanting to uplift more [jIvAthmA](http://ponnadi.blogspot.com/2013/03/tattva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i5631.html)s from samsAram, divinely arranged the appearance of AchAryas starting with [nAthamunigaL](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nathamunigal/) and ending with [maNavALa mAmunigaL (SrI varavaramuni)](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/mamunigal/). [bhagavath rAmAnuja](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/emperumanar/) who is an avathAra visEsham (special incarnation) of Adhi sEshan, appears right in the middle of our [AchArya paramparai](http://acharyas.koyil.org/) and he leads this SrIvaishNava sampradhAyam and visishtAdhvaitha sidhdhAntham to great heights. Following the works of great rishis parAsara, vyAsa, dhramida, tanka, etc., he firmly establishes the visishtAdhvaitha principles. He establishes 74 AchAryas as simhAsanAdIpathis and instructs them to bring SrIvaishNavam to everyone who has the desire to learn about emperumAn and gain true knowledge. periya perumAL accepts maNavALa mAmunigaL (who is yathi punaravathAram – SrI rAmAnuja’s subsequent avathAram) as his own AchAryan in SrIrangam periya kOyil and completes the AchArya rathnahAram which started from him. After the time of [maNavALa mAmunigaL](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/mamunigal/), his main disciples, famously known as ashta dhik gajangaL (eight elephants) headed by [ponnadikkAl jIyar](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/ponnadikkal-jiyar/) further propagate the SrIvaishNava sampradhAyam everywhere. Subsequently, many AchAryas appear in this sampradhAyam and continue the great work of our [pUrvAchAryas](http://acharyas.koyil.org/).


One of the greatest and unique gifts/wealth our [AzhwArs/AchAryas](http://acharyas.koyil.org/) have left behind for us is the amount of divine granthams which are full of true knowledge and which focusses only on the upliftment of us (the jIvAthmAs who are bound in this samsAram). It is very difficult to grasp the main principles from the vast vedam/vedAntam and complicated purANams/ithihAsams (due to some seemingly contradictory statements). On the other hand, our pUrvAchArya SrIsUkthis are made with the intent to clearly deliver the important principles such as supremacy of SrIman nArAyaNan, our svarUpam, our relationship with him, the ultimate goal, the process to reach the goal and the hurdles in accomplishing the goal and method to avoid those hurdles. No other sampradhAyam has such unbroken lineage of AchAryas and such variety of divine literature.

Let us see a comprehensive list of all the srIsUkthis (divine literature) of our [AzhwAr/AchAryas](http://acharyas.koyil.org/). It is unfortunate that we don’t get a lot of these granthams any more. Some of them were lost during the invasions that happened in south India. But, even if we study the granthams which are available now (which are still plenty), we will definitely be greatly benefitted by the spiritually potent content of these granthams.
