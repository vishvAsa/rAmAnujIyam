A parallel between Heidegger’s Gelassenheit and tenkalai śrīvaiṣṇava concept of prapatti & nirhetuka kṛpā:

To set the context, we’ll quickly introduce few concepts.
“Looking something as it is” vs “Looking something as”.

The former is about dealing something purely on its own terms without any external baggage. The term yāthātathya perhaps comes close. However, this isn’t possible because everyone has inherent bias due to inherited context. When you see a being, you’re not seeing the “Being” but through an interpretive lens.

Heidegger calls it Vor-Struktur - The forestructure of understanding, the inescapable mental matrix you bring when engaging with something. It’s components are

vorhabe - fore-having, your the cultural and historical baggage you inevitably bring even before engaging with something.

Vorsicht - (fore-sight); The lens/angle/agenda with which you view everything.

To give an example, if you’re from advaita sampradAya, the upaniShads obviously teach advaita; or if you’re a nArAyaNaparavAdin, the entire veda speak only of nArAyaNaparatva.

Vorgriff - Fore-conception, the set of vocabulary and concepts you bring with you. This is the “Als-struktur” or “as-structure” where you don’t look something as it is but always “looking something from a point of view - looking it as”.

It would seem that “seeing something as it is” is quite impossible in Heideggerian view.

We can table Hirschian strategy of probabilistic reconstruction of authorial intent for now. That’s an interesting topic in itself.

Heidegger proposes “Gelassenheit zu den Dingen”, releasement toward things together with “Offenheit für das Geheimnis”, openness to the mystery.

Gelassenheit is when a being’s “Being” is revealed when you let go of all the interpretive coloring from inherited baggage you bring. It is NOT an active effort of your will. It is stopping your effort of forcing an analytical or willful framework onto reality.

Gelassenheit is a state of open waiting that allows being to manifest on its own terms- i.e. moving away from willful striving to a receptive letting-be. If you decide, “I shall now practice Gelassenheit so that Being reveals itself to me”, you’ve already converted disclosure into something to be produced through an act of will, the very attitude Heidegger is questioning. Gelassenheit is therefore not a technique for extracting a revelation but relinquishing the demand that whatever appears must appear on your terms.

Coming to tenkalai theology, prapatti maps to Gelassenheit - stepping aside to let the truth reveal itself. It’s not an active checklist of actions you perform and then demand moksha. It is the cessation of ego drive effort and be in the state of Heideggerian “receptive waiting” so that the nirhetuka kṛpā which is always there to reveal itself and flow to you.

In both systems, the act of your will only muddy the waters. in both systems, “letting be” isn’t about a descent into unconscious, rock-like insensibility (jaDatva), but an intellectual/spiritual maturity to step aside.

not a descent into unconscious, rock-like insensibility (jaDatva)

Is this then a descent into pashutva, or perhaps ajIvikatva - TW?

Tenks may be said to have “openness to the mystery” beyond “stopping one’s effort” which ajIvika-s show. But then that can’t be said to be any greater than what proper prapanna-s or even post-sannyAsa shAnkara-s have.

The summary of Heideggerian gelassenheit, “what matters most is that (it) cannot be brought about by the subject’s willing.” can very well be the summary of TK’s prapatti position.

Declaration for transparency- I’m more of a Hirschian.
