## कैशिक-नाटकम्
[2026](https://youtu.be/4GUy3LJ-DK0?si=-016BGed02rcDei9)

Towards the end of the nearly 4 hour ritual re- enactment of KAISIKA NATAKAM, the formerly ferocious Bhrama Rakshasan breaks down and pleads for a speck of the Punyam ( blessing) gained by the devotee Nanbaduvan who sang whole heartedly in praise of Nambi Vishnu.

It is a very emotional moment that brings tears to everyone watching.

Cultural historian Madhusudhanan Kalaichelvan takes on the role of the cursed demon in the second part of the play.
