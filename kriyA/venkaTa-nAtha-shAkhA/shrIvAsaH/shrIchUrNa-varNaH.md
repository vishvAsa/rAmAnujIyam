## Provenance
Among vadakalais red vs yellow was more of a deZAchAra & depends on their family practices. Those in thondai & chola desa mostly have yellow. Karnataka , pandya belt and pockets in cholamandala sport red. 

## parakAla-maTha
story is that vks in thiruvaheendrapuram banned vks with red srichurna from participating in any of the temple rites - so krishna tatacharya and the parakala matha head wrote tracts supporting usage of red srichurna by vks. Sri Krishna Brahmatantra Svatantra Jiyar sported red and wrote a work on it (srI churNa chandrika). idk what the color was pre KBTS - but I presume both were allowed.

also interesting fact is there was a tussle between krishna brahmatantra jeeyar and his successor abhinava ranganatha on this. the latter refused to put red color inspite of the former arguing with him. from abhinava ranganatha onwards they changed color to yellow in PM. post him all had yellow sri churna - hence only the oldest gen has red color - and the rest have changed to yellow ever since - even among descendents of who had red.

the 38th or 39th AS of ahobila matha too sported the red colour. 

## kAnchI
Even kanchipuram people call the red color as "theettu srichurna" - apparently they have this practise of wearing this red colour srichurna only during periods of Ashaucha 😂 - now idk whether this is to ragebait tks or seriously has pramana. I am inclined towards the former lol
